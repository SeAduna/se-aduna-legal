Privacy Policy — Se Adună

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · applies to Se Adună version 1.1.1

This Privacy Policy describes how the Se Adună mobile app (the “App”) works from the point of view of user data. The App helps you see mathematically what your spending habits cost over time, track your budget and receipts, take a moment to reflect before a purchase, and keep a record of things you chose not to buy, based on the data you enter yourself.

1. Who operates the App

The App is published by an individual, not by a company.

  • Operator name: Alexandru Dan
  • The postal address is not publicly displayed; contact is by e-mail
  • Contact e-mail: OADsupport@gmail.com

2. What data the App stores on your device

The App has no user account, login, password or mandatory e-mail. There is no server of its own, no online database and no cloud synchronization. The data you enter or create in the App is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Habits: the name, price, frequency, currency and any savings goals;
  • Budget: monthly income, expenses and savings target;
  • My Receipts: the photo of the receipt and the receipt data (merchant, date, total, currency), entered by you or, on iPhone, filled in through text recognition (see Section 4);
  • Micro-Journal: one entry per day, with an outcome chosen from a list, an optional reason chosen from a list and an optional amount; there is no free text;
  • Real Work: monthly net income, hours worked per week and currency, used for an arithmetic calculation;
  • Memory Graveyard: the name, price, currency and date of a memory, an optional reason chosen from a list and an optional photo;
  • Impulse Pause: the amount and category you enter, the answers to the questions and your choice are not saved; they disappear when you leave the screen;
  • Settings: the app language and currency.

Text and numeric data are kept using the App’s standard local storage mechanism (AsyncStorage), and photos are kept as files in the App’s private space, separately for receipts and for memories. Photos are not saved to your phone’s gallery. This data:

  • is not sent to any server of the operator, because there is no server;
  • is not sent to any third party;
  • is not received by and not visible to the operator: the operator has no technical access to it.

Important distinction: the fact that data is stored locally does not automatically mean there is no personal data. It means that the operator does not receive or collect this data, because it does not leave the device through the App. You own and control the data on your own phone.

3. Camera and photos

The App uses the device camera for two features:

  • photographing a receipt (My Receipts);
  • the optional photo of a memory (Memory Graveyard).

The camera permission is requested by the operating system when you choose to take a photo. You can refuse or revoke it at any time in your phone settings. Memories can be saved without a photo; adding a receipt in the App starts from a photo, so without camera access you cannot add new receipts.

The App does not access your phone’s gallery or photo library and does not request that permission. It does not request storage, microphone, location or contacts permissions. Photos taken through the App are kept only in the App’s private space.

4. Text recognition from receipts (OCR)

  • iPhone (iOS): after you take a photo of a receipt, the text is recognized on the device, using the iOS text recognition feature (Apple Vision). The image and the text are not sent by the App to any server; the result appears in the form and you can correct it.
  • Android: the App does not automatically recognize the text on a receipt. You enter the receipt data (merchant, date, total) manually.

5. What the App does NOT use

The App does not use or integrate:

  • analytics or usage-behavior measurement;
  • advertising or advertising SDKs;
  • tracking across apps or websites;
  • financial, banking or payment APIs;
  • access to your bank account or cards;
  • social networks or automatic sharing;
  • push notifications;
  • microphone, location or contacts;
  • access to the photo gallery.

The App has no sharing feature of its own. If you want to show someone a result, you can take a screenshot with your phone’s standard function — an action of yours, outside the App.

6. Permissions and network

The only permission the App explicitly requests is the camera. On Android, the INTERNET permission is excluded from the App (verified in the package manifest); the package may still contain technical permissions added automatically by standard components (for example reading the network state), which do not allow data to be transmitted. The App does not make network requests to external servers and does not transmit your content to the operator or to third parties.

7. Phone backups

Backups are made by the operating system or your account’s backup service (for example iCloud or Google), not by the App, and they are not a transmission of data to the operator. Depending on your phone and account settings, the data stored locally by the App, including receipt and memory photos, may be included in those backups. On Android, the App allows the system’s automatic backup. You can control this in your phone or account backup settings.

8. Deleting data

  • Habits: you can delete a habit in the App.
  • Memory Graveyard: you can delete a memory (after confirmation); deleting it also removes its photo from the device.
  • Receipts: you can open a saved receipt and permanently delete it (after confirmation); deleting it removes the receipt’s data from local storage and its photo from the device. If you cancel a receipt before saving it, its temporary photo is removed.
  • Budget, Micro-Journal, Real Work: you can edit or replace the data you entered.
  • Uninstalling the App removes the data stored locally by the App, according to the standard behavior of the operating system.

Backups previously made by the operating system or your backup service are not controlled by the App; they are managed from that service’s settings. The operator does not hold a copy of your data, so it cannot remotely delete something it does not have.

9. Children and minors

The App is not specifically directed at children. The operator does not receive data from users, including minors, because the data stays on the device (see Section 2).

10. Legal framework

The App is built to limit data: no account, no server, no transmission of content to the operator. This Policy describes the App’s technical behavior. It is not a declaration of legal compliance or a legal opinion.

11. App stores

Distribution through the App Store and Google Play is also subject to Apple’s and Google’s policies. The privacy declarations in the stores are based on the behavior described in this Policy.
