1. What the App is

Se Adună is a set of personal tools that help you understand and reflect on your spending, based on data you enter. The main features are:

  • calculation of the cost of spending habits (monthly, yearly and over 1/5/10/20 years) and an optional budget module (income, expenses, savings target), with hypothetical mathematical simulations;
  • My Receipts: keeping receipt photos and receipt data (automatic text recognition exists only on iPhone);
  • Impulse Pause: a reflection tool before a purchase; the decision remains yours;
  • Micro-Journal: a quick note of impulses, once a day;
  • Real Work: an arithmetic calculation of the value of an hour of your work, based on the data you enter;
  • Memory Graveyard: a private collection of things you chose not to buy.

The App is free, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.
