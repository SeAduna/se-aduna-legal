Financial Disclaimer — Se Adună

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · applies to Se Adună version 1.1.4

An integral part of the Terms and Conditions of Use of the Se Adună app; it applies to all calculations, projections and simulations shown in the App.

1. The App does not provide financial advice

Se Adună is not a financial, tax, accounting or investment advisory service and is not provided by an authorized financial advisor, bank, investment firm or regulated institution.

The App does not recommend, promote or sell any financial, banking, insurance or investment product.

2. All results are mathematical calculations, not predictions

Every figure shown in the App — monthly cost, yearly cost, cost over 1/5/10/20 years, amount saved through a reduction, simulated value of an invested saving — is the result of a simple mathematical formula applied to the data you enter.

  • they are not predictions of what will happen to your money;
  • they are not guaranteed and do not reflect the actual performance of any product, account or financial instrument;
  • they rely on the simplified assumption that the price, frequency and chosen rate remain constant throughout the period.

3. Hypothetical returns are not promises

Where the App lets you choose a “hypothetical return” (for example 5% or 7% per year), that percentage:

  • is chosen by you from a list of predefined scenarios, for illustration only;
  • does not correspond to the actual return of any account, fund, share or other existing instrument;
  • is not a guarantee — actual returns may be lower, equal to zero or negative.

4. Real Work is an arithmetic calculation

The Real Work index is a simple arithmetic calculation made from the data you enter (monthly net income and hours worked per week). It is not financial advice, an assessment of your salary or a recommendation about your work or spending, and the result depends entirely on the data entered.

5. Impulse Pause is a reflection tool

Impulse Pause gives you a moment of pause and a few reflection questions before a purchase. It does not tell you what to buy or not to buy and does not judge whether an expense is good or bad. The decision remains entirely yours.

6. Memory Graveyard and “The value of your choices”

In the Memory Graveyard, “The value of your choices” is the sum of the prices you noted yourself for things you chose not to buy. This value does not represent guaranteed savings, money earned, an investment return or a guaranteed financial benefit. The App does not verify whether you actually kept that money.

7. Responsibility for financial decisions

Any financial decision you make — to reduce, eliminate or continue a habit, to save, to invest or to give up a purchase — belongs entirely to you. The App does not know your full financial situation, your obligations, your complete income or the risks you can take.

Before making important financial decisions, it is advisable to consult an authorized financial professional.

8. The data you enter determines the results

The accuracy of any result depends entirely on the accuracy of the data you enter (price, frequency, income, expenses, hours worked, receipt data). The App cannot verify whether this data is correct or complete.

9. No guarantees of perfect accuracy

To the extent permitted by law, the App is provided “as is”, with no guarantee that the results shown are free of rounding, approximation or data-interpretation errors. The formulas used have been mathematically checked, but we cannot claim absolute accuracy for every combination of data.
